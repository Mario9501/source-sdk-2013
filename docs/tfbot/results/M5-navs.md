## Notes on the unverified maps

Generated by `tools/tfbot/nav_batch.py run`, then re-checked with `run --revalidate` after the validator stopped counting decorative trains as objectives (62 of 81 verified, up from 50; the old verdicts are in the last column). The game prints a console warning on every unverified map; bots still play them.

- **Expected, not fixable by the generator:** `background01` is the menu backdrop (no gameplay). `tc_hydro` enables spawn rooms per round, so from the validator's view 100% of the mesh is cut off.
- **Generation barely sampled the map:** `pl_snowycoast` (71 areas from 257 seeds) and `vsh_outburst` (13 areas). The floors are likely entity brushes or props, which the brush-only generation trace doesn't see. Needs a hand-made nav or a generator change; see the wishlist in DESIGN.md.
- **Routes missing to some objectives** (1–18 each): the remaining arena, CTF, PD, VSH and payload-race maps. Typical causes are objectives reached only by a jump or drop the generator doesn't connect, team-only doors, and payload-race tracks whose second train sits behind a one-way drop. Bots play these maps but may fail to reach those objectives.
